Privacy Policy

Eric's Scrabble 2/3 Letter Word Trainer · Last updated: August 6, 2026
The short version: This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no logins, and no data sent to any server we control. Everything you do in the app — your scores, streaks, and history — stays on your own device.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to Eric's Scrabble 2/3 Letter Word Trainer, available as a web app and as an iOS/Android app (the "App"). It's a personal, independently developed project — not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Hasbro, Mattel, HarperCollins/Collins, NASPA, or the makers of Words With Friends.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information about you. Specifically:

  • The App does not require or offer account creation, sign-in, or registration of any kind.
  • The App does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.
  • The App does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • The App does not use cookies for tracking purposes.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress — things like your score history, accuracy, streaks, high scores, and a record of words you've missed — using your device's local storage. This data:

  • Stays entirely on your device.
  • Is never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • Is not linked to your identity in any way — we have no way of knowing who you are or what you've stored.
  • Persists only on that specific device/browser. It is not backed up or synced across your devices.
  • Can be cleared at any time using the in-app reset option, or by clearing your browser/app data through your device's settings.

Network requests the App makes

The App connects to the internet for a small number of specific, non-personal purposes:

  • Word list lookups (4+ letter words): the App fetches a public, open-source word list (ENABLE, also used as the official Words With Friends dictionary) from GitHub to check whether a word is valid. This request does not include any information about you — it's simply downloading a public data file.
  • Optional links to Collins Dictionary: if you choose to tap the "double-check on the official list" option, the App opens Collins' own website in your browser or a new tab. That visit is subject to Collins Dictionary's own privacy policy, not ours — we have no visibility into what happens on their site.

Sharing your score

If you use the "Share Score" feature, the App generates an image on your own device and hands it to your device's native share sheet (the same system-level sharing menu used by every app on your phone). We do not receive, store, or have any visibility into what you share or who you share it with — that's entirely between you and whichever app or contact you choose.

Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience word-game trainer and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all. As noted above, no personal data is gathered regardless of the user's age.

Advertising

The App displays advertising, served by Google AdMob. These ads are non-personalized — we do not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) or any cross-app tracking to target ads to you, and the App does not request Apple's tracking permission. AdMob may still use limited technical information (such as general, coarse location inferred from IP address, device type, and app context) to serve ads and to prevent fraud, and to fulfil legal obligations, under Google's own advertising policies and privacy policy. We do not control what data AdMob itself collects to serve ads and encourage you to review Google's policies directly for details.

If we introduce personalized advertising in the future, this policy will be updated in advance, and (for iOS) the App will request your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency system before doing so.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Since the App doesn't collect contact information from users, we're not able to notify you directly — we'd encourage checking back here occasionally if you have concerns.
